Sound effects used by sounds.js.

Provided by the site owner
  bike-bell.mp3: the bike's bell ringing as he rides by
  helicopter-loop.wav: a seamless 10-second loop made from the owner's helicopter.mp3

Kenney (www.kenney.nl), Creative Commons Zero (CC0), free to use with no credit required.
They were trimmed, mixed to mono and level-matched for the site.
  "Impact Sounds" pack: step-*, tiptoe-*, hop, tin-*, metal, glass-*, plank, wood-*,
  plate, bit-*, thud, punch, stomp, ding
  "RPG Audio" pack: cloth-*, creak-*, click, latch

The "Hello!" when the character waves is spoken by the browser's built-in voice.
